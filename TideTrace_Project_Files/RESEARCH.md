# TideTrace // Scientific Research & Operational Bounds

---

## 1. Remote Sensing Constraints
- **Bragg Wave Suppression:** Oil films suppress capillary-gravity waves, lowering backscatter by 4.5 to 10 dB.
- **Wind Speed Operational Limits:** Strictly bounded between 3.0 m/s (below which calm sea produces false dark spots) and 12.0 m/s (above which wave breaking submerges the oil slick).

---

## 2. Statutory Legal Boundaries
- **Decision Support:** TideTrace provides probabilistic evidence for Coast Guard interception. Physical verification, oil fingerprinting, and Oil Record Book inspection are required prior to statutory penal action.
